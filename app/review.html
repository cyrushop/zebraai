    How the numbers are worked out
    • POS value is net cost in USD at planned rate. The 2024 export has no net column, so it is SIP after rebate ÷ (1 − sell-out rebate %); this reproduces the 2025 net column exactly.
    • Quarters and weeks are Zebra fiscal ones (year from the Sunday nearest 1 Jan, 13-week quarters; 2026 Q4 = 4 Oct 2026 – 2 Jan 2027), inferred from the POS week and invoice-date pairs. "Same weeks" compares fiscal weeks up to the latest week loaded for this year.
    • Pipeline: Closed-Won, Secure, Validate, Qualify and Identify lines, by the quarter of the schedule date (first-ship quarter when there is none), for the selected primary distributor.
    • Partner status compares this year to date with the same weeks last year: growing ≥ +10%, declining ≤ −10%. New means no POS in earlier loaded years; lapsed means none this year.
    • Sell-in comes from the DSR: lines shipped to the distributor with an invoice date in the fiscal quarter, plus open orders scheduled to ship by quarter end (past-due and undated included). "To order" is Won, Secure and Validate pipeline the order check could not find in the DSR or in VAD stock.
    • Plan: sell-out is compared on POS net (Disti Net) and gross. Projection = QTD + trailing 13-week weekly POS × weeks left. Projected ending inventory = plan starting inventory + sell-in in hand − projected gross sell-out. DOI = inventory ÷ (gross sell-out per day).
    • Stock: free = on hand − committed − reseller backlog that is not on a Zebra open order in the DSR. Run rate is sell-out units over the latest 13 POS weeks; weeks of cover = free ÷ weekly run rate. Stocking gaps use run-rate (no PC) units only.
    • Files stay in this browser (IndexedDB), shared with the order check page. Nothing is uploaded.
