    How the files are combined

    • Sales is SIP / net amount after sell-out rebate, in USD: the one value every year's export carries.
    • Four layouts are recognised from their headers: BI extract (2021–2022), POS detail 2022, POS detail 2023–2024 and FY25 POS detail.
    • In a workbook, only the largest data sheet is read; smaller sheets with the same columns are pivot drill-downs.
    • When two files cover the same year, the one with more quarters (then more lines) is used. The file bar lists what was used and skipped.
    • Product lines are grouped into seven categories. Desktop, Tabletop and Mobile are barcode printers; RFID and RFID Devices are RFID.
    • Negative lines are credits or returns and are kept, so totals are net.
    • BI extracts use standardised end-user names and POS detail files use raw names, so customer counts and new/lost figures across that change are not like-for-like. Sales totals are.
